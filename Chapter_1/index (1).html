Perfect Pan-Seared Steak

This simple steak recipe creates a juicy, flavourful crust using only a few ingredients. It’s quick to prepare and perfect for anyone who loves a rich, restaurant-style steak at home.

Ingredients

  • 1 ribeye or sirloin steak
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 2 garlic cloves (crushed)
  • Fresh rosemary or thyme (optional)

Method

  1. Pat the steak dry with paper towels to help it sear properly.
  2. Season both sides generously with salt and black pepper.
  3. Heat a pan over high heat until very hot.
  4. Place the steak in the pan and sear for 2–3 minutes on each side.
  5. Add butter, garlic, and herbs, then spoon the melted butter over the steak.
  6. Cook to your preferred doneness, then let the steak rest for 5 minutes.
  7. Slice and serve while warm.
