Breaking Bad

A critically acclaimed crime drama series.

Visit Official Website

Plot Overview

Breaking Bad follows Walter White, a high school chemistry teacher who turns to manufacturing methamphetamine after being diagnosed with cancer. Partnering with former student Jesse Pinkman, Walter enters the criminal underworld, leading to dangerous consequences.

Key Information

Release Year: 2008

Created by: Vince Gilligan

Genre: Crime, Drama, Thriller

Cast & Creative Team

Bryan Cranston — Walter White

Aaron Paul — Jesse Pinkman

Anna Gunn — Skyler White

Director/Creator: Vince Gilligan

Main Characters

Walter White — A chemistry teacher turned drug manufacturer.

Jesse Pinkman — Walter’s former student and business partner.

Skyler White — Walter’s wife who becomes suspicious of his actions.

Reviews

Breaking Bad is widely regarded as one of the greatest TV series of all time, praised for its writing, acting, and character development.
